🌸 **One last change I'd make to our earlier plan:** I would **build the Investigation Notebook before SHADOW AI**.

Reason:

* The Notebook becomes the **single source of truth** for an investigation.
* SHADOW AI reads from the Notebook.
* The Report Generator also reads from the Notebook.
* Future features (notes, timeline, evidence, attachments) naturally belong there.

So the refined order becomes:

**File Analysis → Quick Lookups → Investigation Notebook → SHADOW AI → Report Generator → UI Polish**

That keeps the architecture clean, avoids rework, and gives SHADOW a workflow that scales as new analysis modules are added. 😤💙
